Krupali Tejani

I’m passionate about data and the moment when a pile of numbers starts to make sense. I’ve been working with data and visualization since undergrad, and I’ve always enjoyed figuring out what the data is really saying.

Most of my work happens before the chart: building pipelines, cleaning and connecting data, and making sure the numbers actually mean what we think they mean. I enjoy that part just as much as the final visualization, because a chart is only as good as the data behind it. I like turning messy data into something clear, useful, and easy for others to understand.
